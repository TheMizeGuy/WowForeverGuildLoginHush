First release, for World of Warcraft: Forever (1.60.x).

- Hide the "has come online" chat line about guild members.
- Hide the "has gone offline" chat line about guild members.
- Mute the login sound, the chime the game plays when a guild member or character friend comes online.

Each is its own setting, all three on by default: `/glh`, the addons button by the minimap, or Esc > Options > AddOns > Guild Login Hush. Lines about friends who are not in your guild are left alone, and nothing is hidden while you are not in a guild.
